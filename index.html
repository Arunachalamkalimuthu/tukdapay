TukdaPay

Split a UPI payment above ₹2000 into parts of ₹1999 or less, then pay them one by one from GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app.

Max per payment

    Each Pay button opens your UPI app with the amount and note filled in. This page can't see whether a payment went through, so tick each one off yourself. On a computer, scan the QR with your phone instead.

    Questions

    What does TukdaPay do?
    It takes one amount and breaks it into several UPI payments that each stay at or under a limit you choose (₹1999 by default). Each part gets its own Pay link or QR code.
    Does it move money?
    No. TukdaPay only prepares upi://pay links. Your own UPI app makes every payment, and this page never sees your bank details.
    Which UPI apps work?
    Any app that handles UPI links: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay, CRED and bank apps. On a computer, scan the QR code with your phone.
    Can I change the ₹1999 limit?
    Yes. Open "Max per payment" above the Split button and set whatever limit applies to you.
    Is it free and open source?
    Yes. MIT licensed on GitHub. No signup, no ads, no tracking.
