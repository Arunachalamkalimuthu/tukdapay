<Lede>You try to pay a bill above ₹2,000 by UPI and it won’t go through. That can happen for a few different reasons, and each has a different fix. Start with the message on your screen.</Lede>

## First, read the message your app shows

Before you try again, read what the app says. Does it mention a QR code, a new device, your bank, a credit card or a limit? Does it talk about this one payment, or a total for the day? The sections below follow those clues.

If the payment failed but you think money left your account, check your bank app or statement before you pay again, so you don’t pay twice.

## Paying from a QR code in your gallery or a screenshot

Picking the shop’s QR code from your photos, instead of scanning it with the camera, is a different kind of payment. NPCI calls it “QR share and pay”, and in April 2025 [Business Standard reported](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/upi-transactions-through-qr-share-pay-method-discontinued-abroad-125041100859_1.html) an NPCI circular that limits it for payments to offline merchants that aren’t verified. If your message mentions a QR from your gallery, start here.

What to try:

- **Scan the QR code with your UPI app’s camera**, from the sticker at the counter.
- **Type the shop’s UPI ID** instead. It’s usually printed under the QR code. Check the name your app shows before you enter your PIN.
- **If the shop sent the QR code on WhatsApp**, ask them for their UPI ID.

If someone you don’t know sent you a QR code to pay, stop and check who it belongs to before you pay anything.

## Paying from a link instead of a QR

Some apps won’t accept a payment opened from a link, for example to a personal UPI ID. If yours declines a payment you opened from a link (like a TukdaPay Pay button), scan the shop’s QR in your UPI app and type the amount instead.

## New to UPI, a new phone or a new UPI PIN

Banks can limit payments for a while after you register for UPI, or set or reset your UPI PIN. [Paytm’s help page on UPI limits](https://paytm.com/support/upi/upi-transaction-limit-maximum-amount-paytm) mentions a temporary limit some banks apply to new UPI users, and SBI explains its own limit after you set or reset a UPI PIN on its [YONO blog](https://sbi.bank.in/web/yono/blog/understand-upi-transaction-limits-cooling-period-and-payment-tips). Canara Bank’s [UPI terms](https://www.canarabank.bank.in/pages/term-conditions-upi) limit payments after a fresh registration, and ask you to register again when you change phones.

What to try:

- Check your bank’s app or customer care for the limit and when it ends.
- If the payment can wait, make it after that.
- If it can’t, ask the shop about another way to pay.

## Your bank’s UPI limit

The app you pay with isn’t always the one setting the limit. Paytm’s page says the limit is set by your bank, not the UPI app, and [PhonePe’s UPI terms of use](https://www.phonepe.com/terms-conditions/upi/) say limits can be set by PhonePe, by you, by your bank or other payment participants, or by law.

Where to see yours:

- **Google Pay:** [Standard UPI transaction limits](https://support.google.com/pay/india/answer/7359498?hl=en) in Google Pay Help.
- **Paytm:** [What is the maximum amount I can send via UPI?](https://paytm.com/support/upi/upi-transaction-limit-maximum-amount-paytm)
- **BHIM:** the [BHIM FAQ](https://www.bhimupi.org.in/faq-s), under *Sending Money*. Confirm the figure with your bank, which sets the limit for your account.
- **Your bank:** its app, internet banking or customer care has the figure for your account.

Find out two things: whether the limit is **per payment** or **per day**, and whether it counts the **amount** or the **number of payments**. That decides whether paying in parts can help.

## Paying with a RuPay credit card

If you chose a RuPay credit card as the account to pay from, the card’s own limits come in. RuPay’s [FAQs](https://www.rupay.co.in/rupay-credit-card-on-upi) say transactions are subject to “the available credit limit on the card and any transaction limits set by the issuer bank or the customer”, and not every shop accepts credit cards on UPI.

Try paying from your bank account instead, and read [what to check when a RuPay credit card on UPI stops at ₹2,000](/blog/rupay-credit-card-upi-2000-limit/).

## Paying more than ₹2,000 in parts

If the limit you’ve hit is your bank’s limit on a single payment, and the shop agrees, you can pay the bill as a few smaller UPI payments. [TukdaPay’s UPI payment splitter](/) works out the parts and opens each one in your UPI app, with its part number in the note so the shop can match them to the bill.

- **Ask the shop first.** They’ll see several payments instead of one.
- **Only pay UPI IDs you trust.** Take the ID from the shop’s own sticker or bill, and check the name your app shows before each PIN.
- **Don’t pay a QR from your gallery in parts.** Scan the code at the counter or type the shop’s UPI ID instead (see above).

<Callout>
**Parts don’t help with a per-day limit.** If your bank, card or app counts everything you pay in a day, the parts add up to the same total and stop at the same point.
</Callout>
