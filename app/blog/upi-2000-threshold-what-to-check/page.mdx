"₹2000" comes up a lot in UPI news — as a cap on a benefit, the start of a fee, or a reporting line. Before you change how you pay, check which one is actually in force for you today.

> This page deliberately doesn't state the current rule. Rules have been proposed, denied, revised and staggered by merchant size more than once. The only reliable sources are the ones below.

## Who actually sets UPI rules

- **NPCI** runs UPI. Its circulars set limits, fees and merchant categories. [npci.org.in](https://www.npci.org.in/)
- **RBI** sets the policy NPCI operates under, including anything about charges on digital payments. [rbi.org.in](https://www.rbi.org.in/)
- **Ministry of Finance / CBIC** decide tax questions such as GST. A rumour about "tax on UPI" is only real once it appears in a notification or a press release from them. [pib.gov.in](https://pib.gov.in/)
- **Your bank and your UPI app** tell you what they charge *you*, if anything. Look for a "charges" or "fees" page in the app.

## Three different things "₹2000" can mean

### 1. A fee charged to the merchant

Some proposals attach a merchant discount rate (MDR) to UPI payments above a threshold, usually only for large merchants. The customer is not charged directly. Whether a shop passes it on is between you and the shop.

### 2. A charge to the customer

This is the version that spreads fastest on WhatsApp and has, so far, been denied each time it surfaced. Treat it as false until you can find the notification.

### 3. A limit on a benefit

Some incentive schemes for small merchants apply only to transactions up to ₹2,000. Above that, nothing is charged — the merchant simply doesn't get the incentive.

## A ten-second check before you split

1. Search the exact rumour plus the words *"PIB fact check"*. The government's fact-check unit answers most of these within a day.
2. Open your UPI app's fees page. If it charges you nothing above ₹2,000, splitting saves you nothing.
3. Ask the merchant. If a fee applies to them, they'll know, and they may prefer split payments — or not.
